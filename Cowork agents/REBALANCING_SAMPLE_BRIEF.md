## Tax Implications

Singapore tax-resident, not a US person: **no capital gains tax applies to any of these sales** — do not apply US capital-gains math or "tax-loss harvesting" framing to the AAPL trim or any other trade. The relevant recurring tax cost is **dividend withholding tax** on foreign holdings: 30% on US-listed ETFs/stocks (AAPL, GOOGL, VOO, VTI, BND, AGG) held by a non-US person, versus 15% on Ireland-domiciled UCITS funds (CSPX, VWRA, IWDA, EIMI) under the US-Ireland tax treaty — and CSPX/VWRA's accumulating share classes avoid the distribution event entirely by reinvesting internally. SGX-listed holdings (A35, MBH) are SGD-denominated and their dividends are generally tax-exempt for individual Singapore investors. This is why the equity ETF picks above default to Ireland-UCITS rather than US-listed funds at this position size, and why the fixed-income sleeve defaults to SGX-listed funds.
